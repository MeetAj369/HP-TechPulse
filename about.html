Enterprise ITAM Architecture

System Design & Technology Stack

HP TechPulse replaces fragmented IT spreadsheets with a relational 14-table database, automated financial compliance, cryptographic QR passports, and algorithmic health scoring.

1. Gadget Passport™ & QR Engine

Generates on-demand cryptographic QR codes linked to live hardware profiles, and prints verified certificates with the FPDF engine.

2. Health Intelligence (0–100)

Calculates operational age decay (-30 pts max), repair frequency penalty (-8 pts/repair), open critical fault penalty (-15 pts), and warranty bonuses (+10 pts).

3. WDV Financial Depreciation

Automated 5-year Written Down Value (WDV) accounting schedules and Total Cost of Ownership (CapEx + accumulated OpEx maintenance) modeling.

4. 5-Stage Ticket Lifecycle

Structured IT escalation pipeline (Open → Assigned → In-Progress → Testing → Resolved) with chronological technician timestamps.

5. Green Pulse Sustainability

Carbon footprint metrics and e-waste prevention calculations by extending corporate hardware longevity through proactive maintenance.

6. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Three distinct access tiers for System Administrators, Support Specialists, and End-User Employees with BCrypt password security and audit logs.

14-Table Relational Schema (`techpulse369.sql`)

The system backend is built on a highly normalized MySQL schema:

gadgets: Hardware Master
users: RBAC Credentials
issues: Tickets Registry
issue_updates: Audit Stages
maintenance: Preventative OpEx
warranties: Vendor AMCs
stock: Spares & Inventory
audit_log: Security Trail
